Support Watseka's Future

Watseka Community Renewal is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Your donation is tax-deductible and directly supports small business grants and community-driven projects in Watseka.

Every dollar helps us invest in local businesses, preserve historic structures, and build a stronger community.

About the Community Foundation of Kankakee River Valley

Online gifts are processed through the Community Foundation of Kankakee River Valley, which has been "connecting people who care with causes that matter" across Kankakee and Iroquois Counties since 1982. The Foundation is a trusted local nonprofit that holds and stewards charitable funds for organizations throughout the region. Giving through our fund there means your contribution is handled securely and deposited directly into the Watseka Community Renewal account.
