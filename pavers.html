The Paver Wall

Every engraved brick for the Hubbard Trail mural site

Thank you to everyone leaving their mark on Watseka! Each brick below is a 4" x 8" engraved paver that will be installed at the Hubbard Trail mural, 203 E. Walnut St., in Spring 2027. New names are added as orders are paid. Orders must be received by February 1, 2027.

Order a Brick: $125

Don’t See Your Brick?

Bricks are added once payment is received, so it may take a few days for a new order to appear. Still missing? Call Helen Gerber at 815-383-3361 or email WatsekaCommunityRenewal@gmail.com.

Order a Brick

Commemorate a loved one, celebrate a special occasion, or showcase your family name. Up to 3 lines, 20 characters each, for $125. Orders must be received by February 1, 2027. Fill out the order form to get started.
