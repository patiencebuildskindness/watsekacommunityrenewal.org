New Public Art Project

Hubbard Trail: The Path That Built Watseka

A new wall mural at 203 E. Walnut Street, downtown Watseka

Watseka Community Renewal is bringing a brand-new wall mural to downtown Watseka, celebrating the rich history and enduring spirit of our community. Centered on the theme “Hubbard Trail: The Path That Built Watseka,” this mural will be a vibrant reminder of our shared heritage — and, true to our mission, it’s being built by the community. We’re inviting residents to help bring it to life through voting, volunteering, and donations.

Design Option 2 Is the Winner!

The votes are counted and the community has spoken. Design Option 2 — featuring Watchekee, Gurdon Hubbard, and Catherine Clifton — won with 678 of 1,276 votes. Thank you to everyone who cast a ballot! Sign up below for email updates as the mural comes together, and let us know if you’d like to volunteer.

Thank You for Voting!

The community turned out in a big way — both online and at in-person ballot stations throughout July:

  • Watseka Farmers Market
  • Watseka Public Library
  • Community Meetings — pop-up voting stations at organizations across town

A total of 1,276 votes were counted, and Design Option 2 came out on top. Sign up for email updates to follow the mural from first panel to finished wall.

Get Involved

Volunteer: Help with the physical development and painting of the mural. No professional artistic experience required — just passion for the community and a willingness to help. (Check the volunteer box in the sign-up form below and we’ll be in touch.)

Donate: Contributions cover high-quality paint, weatherproofing, artist fees, and equipment. Give online, or mail a check to Watseka Community Renewal, 103 W. Cherry St., Watseka, IL 60970.

Small Business Grant Program

Through a pilot project, Watseka Community Renewal supported local businesses with small grants for storefront improvements, marketing, and operational growth. In our first funding cycle we awarded five grants of $1,000 each to Watseka businesses. Applications closed on April 1, 2026, and the recipients have been announced.
